3. Common Situations for Errors and Omissions

At any time during the sales process there can be a misunderstanding or misrepresentation that could lead to legal action being taken
by the insured. Agents should document everything: interviews, phone conversations, requests for information, etc. The sales interview
and the policy delivery are the most common occasions for errors and omissions (E&O) situations to occur that may result in providing
inadequate coverage or failure to maintain and service coverage.
