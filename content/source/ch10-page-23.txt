Adoptions

If an insured has coverage for maternity benefits on the date of an adoptive placement, the insured's policy must provide an adoptive
indemnity benefit if a child is placed for adoption within 90 days of birth. If more than | child is placed for adoption from the same birth,
only | adoption indemnity benefit is required. The amount of this indemnity is $4,000, although it is subject to adjustment.

Federal Health Care Reform Required Dependent Coverage

Any family health coverage must provide coverage for newborns from the moment of birth. Newborns adopted by the insured will be
covered from birth if adoption occurs within 30 days of the child's birth, or from the date of placement, if adoption occurs 30 days or
more after the child’s birth. Newborns are covered from the moment of birth without any pre-existing conditions for 30 days. The insured
must pay an additional premium if coverage is required beyond 30 days.

2. Other Required Benefits

Substance Abuse Coverage

Each group accident and health insurance policy must contain an optional rider allowing certificate holders to obtain coverage for
alcohol or drug dependency treatment in programs licensed by the Department of Human Services, inpatient hospitals accredited by
the joint commission on the accreditation of hospitals, or facilities licensed by the Department of Health.
