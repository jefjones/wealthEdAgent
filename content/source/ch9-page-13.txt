5. At-work Benefits

Insurance companies provide some benefits to help insureds that have been disabled get back to work.

Partial Disability Benefit

Partial disability is often defined as the inability to perform one or more of the regular duties of one’s own occupation or the inability to
work on a full-time basis, which results in a decrease in the individual's income. The purpose of the partial disability benefit is to cover a
partial loss of income when the insured is disabled to the point of being able to report to work, but not being able to perform alll of the
regular duties of the job. The partial disability benefit is typically 50% of the total disability benefit, and is limited to a certain period of
time, as noted in the policy.

The benefits paid on a partial disability policy are paid in a flat amount, or a residual amount.
