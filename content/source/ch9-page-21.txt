2. Disability Buy-sell Policy

A buy-sell agreement is legal agreement prepared by an attorney. The buy-sell agreement specifies how the business will pass
between owners when one of the owners dies or becomes disabled. It is common for the business to purchase insurance to provide the
cash to accomplish the buyout when the owner either dies or becomes disabled. The policies that fund buy-sell agreements generally
have an extremely long elimination period, possibly one or two years. Generally, these policies funding buy-sell agreements also
provide a large lump-sum benefit to buy out the business rather than monthly benefits.
