Cash Surrender Value

The policyowner simply surrenders the policy for the current cash value at a time when coverage is no longer needed or affordable.
Upon receipt of the cash surrender value, if the cash value exceeds premiums paid, the excess is taxable as ordinary income. Once this
option is selected, the insured is no longer covered. A policy that has been surrendered for its cash value cannot be reinstated. A
surrender charge is a fee charged to the insured when a life policy or annuity is surrendered for its cash value.
