Replacement
If a long-term care insurance policy or certificate replaces another, the replacing insurance company must waive any time periods
applicable to pre-existing conditions, waiting periods and probationary periods in the new long-term care policy or certificate for
similar benefits to the extent that similar exclusions have been satisfied under the original policy or certificate.
Rate Disclosure Form
Insurers must provide to applicants or enrollees in a group long-term care policy a disclosure form which outlines the potential rate
increases for the long-term policy. The disclosure form must be provided to the applicant at application or enrollment date, or at time of
delivery of the approved policy.
For policies on which the premium rate or rate schedule can be increased, the insurer must provide the following information at the time
of application or enrollment:
* Astatement that the policy may be subject to future rate increases;
* An explanation of potential future premium rate revisions and the policyholder's or certificateholder's options if a revision occurs;
* The applicable premium rate or rate schedules that will be in effect until the insurer requests an increase;
* Ageneral explanation for adjustments that includes a description of when adjustments will be effective and the insurer's right to a
revised premium rate or rate schedule if either is changed;
* Information on each premium rate increase on the policy form or a similar policy form over the past 10 years that identifies at least
the following:
© The policy forms for which premium rates have been increased;
© The calendar years when the form was available for purchase; and
© The amount or percent of each increase expressed as a percentage of the premium rate before increase or minimum and
maximum percentages if the increase is variable by rating characteristics;
* Any explanatory information related to the rate increases may be added by the insurer.
The applicant will sign a form acknowledging the insurer made the required disclosure. The insurer must notify the policyowner of an
upcoming increase in premiums at least 45 days prior to the increase.
