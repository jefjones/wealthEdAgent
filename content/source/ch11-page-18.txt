Utah Mini- COBRA
An employee who participates in a group disability policy is entitled to have an individual policy issued on the same basis of eligibility.
Utah Mini-COBRA allows the employees to extend coverage under the group policy for 12 months. The right to extend coverage includes
voluntary and involuntary termination, retirement, sabbatical or leave of absence, reduction of hours, divorce or legal separation, loss of
dependent status, disability or death.
However, the employee does not have this right if he or she has not been continuously covered under this policy for 3 months prior to
the termination.
In addition, the employer must provide written notification of the right to extend group coverage within 30 days of the termination of
group coverage.
Furthermore, the insurer must provide the employee, spouse or dependent the opportunity to extend group coverage if:

* The employer does not provide the terminated insured with the aforementioned notification; and

« The employee contacts the insurer within 60 days of coverage termination.
This extended coverage may be terminated if the insured:

* Established residence outside the state;

* Moves out of the insurer's service area;

© Fails to pay premiums;

* Commits fraud;

* Makes a misrepresentation;

* Becomes eligible for other, similar coverage; or

The employer's coverage is terminated.
Within 30 days of the exhaustion of any extended coverage, the insured and any dependents must be notified of their right to an
individual conversion policy.
