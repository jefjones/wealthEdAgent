Any Occupation

A policy that has an "any occupation’ provision will only provide benefits when the insured is unable to perform any of the duties of the
occupation for which they are suited by reason of education, training, or experience. “Own occupation’ is the more liberal definition and
therefore provides a better benefit for the insured.

Although some companies still utilize the two-tier approach by combining both definitions in a single disability income policy, from an
underwriting standpoint, it is much easier for an insurance company to justify the "any occupation’ definition when agreeing to issue a
policy.
