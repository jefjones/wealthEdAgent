Residual Disability Benefit

Residual disability is the type of disability income policy that provides benefits for loss of income when a person returns to work after a
total disability, but is still not able to work as long or at the same level he/she worked before becoming disabled. Many companies have
replaced partial disability with residual disability. Residual disability will help pay for loss of earnings. If the person can only work part-
time or at a lesser paying position, residual disability will make up the difference between their present earnings and what they were
earning prior to disability.
