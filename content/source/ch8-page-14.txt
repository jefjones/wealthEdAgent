Hospital Indemnity (Income)

A hospital indemnity policy provides a specific amount on a daily, weekly or monthly basis while the insured is confined to a hospital.
Payment under this type of policy is unrelated to the medical expense incurred, but based only on the number of days confined in a
hospital. This can also be called a hospital fixed-rate policy.

Credit Disability

A credit disability policy is issued only to those in debt to a specific creditor. In case of the borrower's disability, payments to the creditor
will be made on the loan until the disabled borrower is able to return to work.
