8. Pre-existing Conditions
A health benefit plan for small employer may impose a pre-existing condition exclusion if
« The exclusion relates to a pre-existing condition for which medical advice, diagnosis, care or treatment was recommended 6
months prior to enrollment;
* The exclusion period ends no later than 12 months after the enrollment date (or 18 months after late enrollment); and
* The exclusion period is reduced by any days of creditable coverage.
In addition, a health benefit plan may impose a limitation period if:
* Each policy that imposes a limitation period under the health benefit plan specifies the physical condition that it excludes;
* The limitation period does not exceed 12 months; and
* The limitation period is applied uniformly.
9. Surcharge for Charging Carriers
A carrier may charge a small group that moves coverage to it from another carrier a one-time charge of up to 25% of the annualized
premium that the carrier could otherwise charge.
The surcharge may not be imposed if
* The change in carriers occurs on the anniversary of the plan year;
« The previous coverage was terminated; or
* Employees from an existing group form a new business.
