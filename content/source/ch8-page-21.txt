Nature and Purpose
Underwriting is the first step in the total process of insuring health risks. The basic purpose of health insurance underwriting is to
minimize the problem of adverse selection. Adverse selection involves the fact that those most likely to have claims are those who are
most likely to seek insurance. An insurance company that has sound underwriting guidelines will avoid adverse selection more often
than not.
Employee Waiver Form
When an insurer offers a health benefit plan to an individual or small employer, the insurer is allowed to combine the uniform
application form and the uniform waiver form. The uniform waiver of coverage form
* Identifies the employee purchasing the plan;
* Verifies the employee's insurance; and
* Cannot include any health status questions other than for pregnancy.
Disclosure Information about Individuals
