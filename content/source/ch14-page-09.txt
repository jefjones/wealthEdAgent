Key person disability income premiums are not deductible to the business, but the benefits are received income tax free by the

2. Buy-sell Policy

Disability buy-sell insurance is typically written to cover partners or corporate officers of a closely held business. The policy provides
funds for the business organization to purchase the business interest of a disabled partner. in a disability buy-sell policy, whether a
cross purchase or entity, the premiums are not deductible to the business, but the benefits are received income tax free by the
business.
