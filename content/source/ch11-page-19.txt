Conversion Rights
An individual that has lost group coverage may elect conversion coverage with the same insurer, if this person:

* Has been continuously covered for 3 months by the policy;

* Has exhausted Utah mini-COBRA coverage or federal COBRA coverage;

* Isnot covered by any other group coverage, covering all pre-existing conditions; and

* Resides in the insurer's service area.
The application and first premium must be sent to the insurer no more than 60 days after the termination. The initial premium for the
first 12 months of the converted policy must be determined in accordance with premium rates applicable to age, class of risk, and the
type and amount of insurance provided.
Reinstatement of Coverage for Military Personnel
Insurers cannot refuse to reinstate an insured or any dependents whose coverage lapsed due to the insured's mobilization into the
United States armed forces - provided an application is made within 180 days of release from active duty. If this provision is followed,
the reinstated policy will not have a waiting period or exclude any pre-existing conditions. This policy may not excessively increase the
insureds premium.
