Need for Variable License to Recommend Termination
Before a producer recommends the sale or termination of a variable product contract, the producer must be licensed as a variable
contract producer. That means a producer must hold a life insurance license and a securities license (either Series 6 or Series 7 and
63). The producer must also be approved as a Utah resident producer by the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA), Central
Registration Depository (CRD).
POLICIES COMPARED
* Key Features: Can be Term or Whole Life; can convert from one to the other
* Premium: Can be increased or decreased by policyowners
Adjustable Life * Face Amount: Flexible; set by policyowner with proof of insurability
* Cash Value: Fixed rate of return; general account
* Policy Loans: Can borrow cash value
* Key Features: Permanent insurance with renewable term protection component
* Premium: Flexible; minimum or target
Universal Life * Face Amount: Flexible; set by policyowner with proof of insurability
* Cash Value: Guaranteed at a minimum level; general account
* Policy Loans: Can borrow cash value
* Key Features: Permanent insurance
* Premium: Fixed (if Whole Life); flexible (if Universal Life)
Variable Life * Face Amount: Can increase or decrease to a stated minimum
* Cash Value: Not guaranteed; separate account
* Policy Loans: Can borrow cash value
