2. Required Notice to Insured

To make sure that the insured is aware that the policy's benefits are limited, the insurance company must, by law, plainly state the
limited policy notice on the first page of the policy, "THIS IS A LIMITED POLICY.”

3. Types of Limited Policies

There are a variety of health insurance policies providing limited coverage for specific accidents or sickness. These contracts must
specify the type of accident or sickness covered, limited perils and amounts of coverage. Benefits may be paid on an expense-paid
(reimbursement) basis or indemnity basis.
