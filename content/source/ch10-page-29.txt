5. Federal-HIPUtah
Federal-HIPUtah is a temporary high-risk health insurance pool that provides transitional coverage to individuals with pre-existing
conditions who have been without coverage for 6 months. It is a federally subsidized health insurance program that will remain in effect
until 2014, when health insurance exchanges become available, and pre-existing condition exclusions are no longer allowed.
To be eligible for Federal-HIPUtah, an individual must meet the following requirements:

* Be lawfully admitted to or a citizen of the United States;

* Bea Utah resident on the date of application;

* Not be covered under creditable coverage during the 6-month period prior to the date of application for Federal-HIPUtah;

* Have a pre-existing condition and meet the required state health underwriting criteria;

* Pay the established premium; and

* Not be ineligible otherwise.
6. Uniform Health Benefit Plan Information Card
Insurers must use and issue a health benefit plan card for applicants who purchase or renew a health benefit plan. The health benefit
card must include

* Name of the insured;

« Name of the insurer;

* Contact information for the insurer;

* Information on copayments and deductibles; and

* An indication of whether the health benefit plan is regulated by the state.
