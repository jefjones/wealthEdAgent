When the medical caregiver provides services to only members or subscribers of a health organization, and contractually is not allowed
to treat other patients, it is referred to as closed panel. in a closed panel arrangement, the doctors are considered employees of the
health organization.

Types of Parties to the Provider Contract

Any physician or hospital that qualifies and agrees to follow the PPO’s standards and charge the appropriate fees that the PPO has
established can be added to the PPO’s approved list at any time. Physicians and providers may belong to several PPO groups
simultaneously.

Utah NetCare Plan

Utah's NetCare plan offers alternative health coverage for individuals who cannot afford COBRA premiums.

NetCare coverage is available for people who are eligible for the continuation of employer group coverage under federal COBRA laws or
state mini-COBRA, or those who are eligible for a conversion to an individual plan after the exhaustion of benefits under federal COBRA
or state mini-COBRA.

The right to extend coverage applies to any spouse or dependents, including a surviving spouse or dependent whose coverage ends
after the death of the employee.

NetCare is available as an individual plan or group plan for up to 12 months for Utah residents transitioning off their employer-
sponsored health plan. After the initial 12-month period, members would have to reapply as individuals.

NetCare is able to offer lower rates because it doesn't conform to the minimum coverage requirements other Utah health insurance
plans have. This means some health expenses would need to be paid by the insured, including diabetes management, adoption
benefits, and the use of a specialist as a primary care physician.
