Guaranteed Renewable

The guaranteed renewable provision is similar to the noncancellable provision, with the exception that the insurer can increase the
policy premium on the policy anniversary date. The insured, however, has the unilateral right to renew the policy for the life of the
contract. The insurer may increase premiums on a elass basis only and not on an individual policy. As with the noncancellable policy,
coverage generally is not renewable beyond the insured’s age 65. Medicare Supplements and long-term care policies must be
written as guaranteed renewable contracts, and cannot be cancelled by the company at the insured's age 65.
