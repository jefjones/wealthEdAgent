1. Amounts Available to Policyowner

Cash Value Increases

Any cash value accumulations in the policy can be borrowed against by the policyowner, or may be paid to the policyowner upon
surrender of the policy. Cash values grow tax deferred. Upon surrender or endowment, any cash value in excess of cost basis (premium
payments) is taxable as ordinary income. Upon death, the face amount is paid, and there is no more cash value. Death benefits
generally are paid to the beneficiary income tax free.
