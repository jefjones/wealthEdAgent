5. Employer Group vs. Association Group
The Commissioner of Insurance has provided rules to standardize and simplify terms for the public when purchasing group insurance.
These rules apply to all groups authorized to operate in the state. Two such groups are single-employer groups and association groups.
The single-employer group is formed by one employer for the benefit of its employees. Under a single-employer group, an employee
can include

* The owner running the business;

* Partners of a partnership;

* Retired employees;

* Former employees; or

* Directors of a corporation.
The association group, such as an alumni group or professional group (e.g. state teachers’ association), can purchase group insurance
for its members. Association groups normally are required to

* Maintain a minimum of 100 members;

* Have been active for at least 2 years;

* Have a constitution and by-laws; and

* Hold meetings at least annually.
