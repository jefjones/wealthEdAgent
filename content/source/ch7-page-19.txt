Section 1035 Exchanges
In accordance with Section 1035 of the Internal Revenue Code, certain exchanges of life insurance policies and annuities may occur as
nontaxable exchanges. When a policyowner exchanges a cash value life insurance policy for another cash value life insurance policy, or
cash value life policy for an annuity, or an annuity for an annuity, the policies or annuities must be on the same life. There will be no
income tax on these transactions.
The following are allowable exchanges:

* Alife insurance policy for another life insurance policy, an endowment contract, or an annuity contract;

* An endowment contract for another endowment contract or an annuity contract; or

* An annuity contract for another annuity contract.
Note that a policyowner may not exchange funds from an annuity into a cash value life policy. Nor would term life be used in a 1035
Exchange since it has no cash value. The key is that the exchange may not be from a less tax-advantaged contract to a more tax-
advantaged contract. “Same to same" is acceptable.
