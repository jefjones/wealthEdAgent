Young adults under age 30, and individuals who cannot obtain affordable coverage (have a hardship exemption) may be able to

purchase individual catastrophic plans that cover essential benefits. These plans offer lower monthly premiums but also feature high

deductibles (several thousand dollars). The insured is usually required to pay all medical costs up to a certain amount. After the insured

reaches the deductible, costs for essential health benefits will be covered by the catastrophic plan with no copayment or coinsurance.
Know This! Individual catastrophic plans that cover essential benefits are available for adults under age 30 and individuals who
cannot obtain affordable coverage.
