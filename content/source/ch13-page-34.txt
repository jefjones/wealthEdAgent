7. Federal Reform - CLASS Act
The Community Living Assistance Services and Support (CLASS) Act is a voluntary insurance program proposed by Congress. It is
designed to assist those individuals and families with limited resources who require services of a nonmedical nature. The program is
designed to provide that assistance throughout communities in the United States. The services will be paid for through a payroll
deduction on a monthly basis. individuals must be 18 years of age or older to qualify for benefits and must have contributed monthly
premiums to the program for at least 5 years. Working adults will have the option to opt out if they so desire.
8. Qualified LTC Plans
A long-term care insurance policy meets the definition of a qualified long-term care insurance contract if alll of the following are
present:
* The only insurance protection provided under the contract is coverage of qualified long-term care services;
* The contract does not pay or reimburse expenses incurred for services that are reimbursable under Title XVIII of the Social Security
Act;
* The contract is guaranteed renewable;
* All premium refunds and dividends are used to reduce future premiums or increase future benefits to offset inflation; and
« The contract satisfies certain consumer protection provisions concerning model regulation and model act provisions, disclosure,
and nonforfeitability.
Only products that meet the Qualified LTC definitions are eligible to be marketed as Partnership LTC plans.
