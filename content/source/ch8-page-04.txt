Types of Losses and Benefits

1. Loss of Income from Disability

Loss of income caused by accident and/or sickness that results in the insured's inability to work and earn income is covered under
disability income policies or coverages. Disability income insurance is a valued contract or stated amount that pays weekly or monthly
benefits due to an injury or sickness. Benefits may be determined by the insured's past earnings and may be limited to a percentage of
that income.

2. Medical Expense

A medical expense contract covers many of the expenses one incurs from an accident or sickness, such as a physician or hospital
expense. Expenses may be paid directly to the insured, and the insured would be responsible for paying the medical expenses. This type
of benefit payment is called reimbursement. if expenses are paid on a scheduled basis, the insurance company will refer to a list
determining the cost of the treatment, and it will only pay up to a certain amount. If a person were covered as a dependent under their
spouse's group insurance, payment of medical expenses would be coordinated.
