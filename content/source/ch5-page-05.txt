3. Insurance Aspects of Annuities

Annuities are not life insurance; they do not pay a face amount upon the death of the annuitant. In fact, they are just the opposite. In
most cases, the payment phase stops upon the death of the annuitant. Annuities do use a mortality table, but this table reflects a
longer life expectancy than the table used in life insurance. A deferred annuity does grow tax-deferred.
