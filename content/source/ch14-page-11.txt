2. Health Reimbursement Accounts (HRAs)
In Health Reimbursement Accounts (HRAs), the employer's contribution is tax deductible in the year in which the reimbursement is
made to the employee. The employee is not taxed on receipt of the benefit. Benefits must be paid solely to the employee for medical
care expenses for the employee, the employee's spouse or dependents. If funds are distributed for other than medical care expenses,
the benefit is considered to be taxable income to the employee.
