Indemnity Plans

1. Choice of Providers

Some dental plans limit the insured’s choices of providers, but others simply limit the benefits to any qualified practitioner.

2. Scheduled vs. Nonscheduled Plans

Basic or scheduled plans pay benefits from a list of procedures up to the amount shown in the schedule. Most plans provide first-dollar
benefits without coinsurance or deductibles. Maximum benefits are often lower than the usual and customary charges of dentists who
force the insured to bear a portion of the cost.
